APEX HEALTH INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED
POLICY TERMS AND CONDITIONS - RETAIL HEALTH SHIELD

This Policy records the agreement between Apex Health Insurance Company Limited ("Insurer") and the Insured Person. Subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions contained herein, the Insurer undertakes to pay admissible hospitalization expenses incurred during the Policy Period up to the Sum Insured.

1. DEFINITIONS
- "Accident": A sudden, unforeseen, and involuntary event caused by external, visible, and violent means.
- "Hospitalisation": An admission into a registered Hospital for a continuous period of not less than 24 consecutive hours, except for specified Day Care Treatments.
- "Day Care Treatment": Medical treatment or surgical procedure undertaken under general or local anesthesia in a hospital in less than 24 hours due to technological advancements.
- "Pre-existing Disease (PED)": Any condition, ailment, or injury diagnosed or for which medical advice or treatment was received within 36 months prior to the first policy inception.

2. SCOPE OF COVER & SUB-LIMITS
The Insurer will cover medically necessary expenses under the following heads up to the base Sum Insured:
a) Room, Board & Nursing Expenses: Covered up to 1% of the Sum Insured per day.
b) Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Charges: Covered up to 2% of the Sum Insured per day.
c) Medical Practitioners & Surgeon Fees: Covered up to 25% of the Sum Insured per illness or injury.
d) Ambulance Expenses: The company will reimburse licensed ambulance transportation costs up to a maximum of INR 2,000 per hospitalization.
e) Cataract Treatment: Limited to 10% of the Sum Insured, subject to a maximum cap of INR 25,000 per eye.
f) AYUSH / Alternative Treatment: Inpatient care taken under Ayurvedic, Homeopathic, or Unani systems is covered up to INR 20,000 per policy year at recognized government institutions.
g) Free Health Check-up: Available after every 3 consecutive claim-free policy years, reimbursed up to 1% of the Sum Insured, capped at a maximum of INR 2,500.

3. WAITING PERIODS
- Initial Waiting Period: A waiting period of 30 days from the policy inception date applies to all illnesses, except hospitalization necessitated by an accidental bodily injury.
- Specific Illness Waiting Period: A 24-month waiting period applies for treatments of Cataract, Hernia, Hydrocele, Piles, Sinusitis, and Joint Replacement.
- Pre-existing Diseases (PED): Covered only after 36 consecutive months of continuous coverage under this policy without any break.

4. CLAIMS PROCEDURE
- Claim Intimation: Hospitalization must be intimated to the insurer within 48 hours of admission for emergency treatments, and at least 48 hours prior for planned admissions.
- Document Submission: All original discharge summaries, itemized bills, prescriptions, and diagnostic reports must be submitted within 15 days from the date of discharge.
- Co-Payment: A 10% co-payment applies to all admissible claims if treatment is taken in a Non-Network Hospital.

5. MAJOR EXCLUSIONS
No indemnity is available for:
- Cosmetic or aesthetic surgery, plastic surgery, or gender reassignment procedures.
- Treatments related to intentional self-injury, suicide attempts, or substance/alcohol abuse.
- Dental treatment or surgery unless necessitated by an accidental injury.
- Expenses for spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids, wheelchairs, and external prosthetics.
- Investigative check-ups without active inpatient treatment.
